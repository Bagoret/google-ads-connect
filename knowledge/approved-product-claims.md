## Prohibited or restricted wording
Defaults to apply until the business supplies its own list:
- Medical/therapeutic claims (treats, cures, heals, eliminates acne/eczema, etc.).
- Absolute or unverifiable claims ("100% efficace", "le meilleur", "miracle")
  unless listed as approved with evidence.
- "Sans [ingredient]" / "free-from" claims unless approved for that product.
- Before/after promises, guaranteed results, specific timeframes ("en 7 jours").
- Brand-owner trademarks in ad text where the brand has not authorised it.
