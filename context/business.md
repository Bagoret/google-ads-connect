## Brand classification (used to segment queries and campaigns)
Definitions used throughout this system:
- **Marketplace brand** — searches/campaigns about the shop's own name.
- **Stocked brand** — searches/campaigns about third-party brands the shop sells.
- **Generic / non-brand** — category or need-based searches with no brand.
- **Competitor retailer** — other shops' names (not a brand the shop stocks).
